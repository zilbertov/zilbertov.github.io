Atlas Backups — Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026.

This private application uses Google OAuth to access the account owner's Google Drive with the drive.file permission. It manages backup files created or explicitly used through the application, according to the owner's backup and retention configuration.

Backup contents are encrypted locally with Restic before transmission to Google Drive. Recovery keys are kept separately under the owner's control. OAuth credentials are stored in access-restricted configuration files and are used only to authenticate backup operations.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use backup contents to train AI models. It does not disclose decrypted backup contents to model providers or operate a public data-sharing service. Storage and transport use the owner's chosen infrastructure and Google Drive.

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings, remove its stored OAuth credentials and delete backup files from Google Drive. Deletion and retention changes can remove recovery points.

This static informational site has no application-level analytics, advertising, cookies or user-submission forms. Its hosting provider and Google services process their own operational data under their respective policies. Questions and access decisions are handled by the owner operating this private installation.
