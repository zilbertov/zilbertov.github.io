Atlas Backups — Terms of use

Effective date: 16 October 2026.

Atlas Backups is a private backup utility operated for the account owner. This website describes that installation; it does not offer public access, paid subscriptions, file hosting or account creation.

The owner chooses which data to back up, supplies authorized Google access, controls encryption and recovery keys, and selects retention and deletion settings. The utility accesses only Google Drive files used with the app through the drive.file permission.

A backup is considered verified only after successful integrity and recovery checks. Revoking access, removing keys or deleting retained files can prevent recovery. These informational pages do not change the applicable terms of Google services or the licenses of the underlying backup tools.
